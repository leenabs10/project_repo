Empty/loaded equipment contrast: completed
=========================================


Purpose
Test whether paired equipment examples reduce false goods detections without
regressing recall. This is a new matched protocol, not an improvement claim or
a continuation of the previous rejected runs. Existing results remain preserved.

Training-only diagnosis and real-data coverage
The retained real-replay predictions were recounted on all 30 training frames
for seeds 17, 29 and 43, with exact RGB/target/prediction checksum agreement.
Both existing background-only references, 000482 and 000880, have zero false
positive pixels in all three seeds. This measures fitting to known references;
it does not demonstrate generalization to other empty equipment.
Largest mean training FP counts are 000624 (6688), 000426 (6070), 000538 (4659)
and 000397 (4269). Binary target errors are not equipment-specific rates.
Diagnostic overlays retain ignored pixels and do not alter any annotation.

Seventy-two training-only RGB previews were inspected. Eight varied views are
quarantined as possible annotation candidates. They span shelving, tyre loads,
access structures, pallets, handling equipment, robots and close cartons. They
are still dependent views from the same trajectory, not eight independent new
scenes. Their usefulness beyond the existing references needs adjudication;
additional distinct real recordings remain necessary. No new real target was
invented or admitted, and no development/test image selected these candidates.

Prepared synthetic batch
Three pairs, six native 1280x720 captures:
- close pallet: empty versus four visible tyres;
- four stacked pallets: empty versus two cartons, partly cropped by the camera;
- rack-side pallet: empty versus tyres partly occluded by the rack upright.
Equipment, structure, camera, lights and materials are identical within a pair.
Only the cargo is added. Cargo shadows can legitimately change other RGB pixels.
All old shelf stock is removed in both members so that empty masks contain only
background. Loaded masks mark visible cargo only; supports and occluders stay
background. The four earlier rack-composition candidates remain separate.

The close tyre load occupies 13.13% of the training image and its display-dark
fraction is 44.02%; the occluded load occupies 2.87% and is 56.35% dark. Dark means
Pillow luminance below 50 after BOX resampling to 640x360. These observations
are not calibrated appearance matching, sensor realism or evidence of recall gain.
One authored aisle, repeated assets and simple pallet geometry remain limits.
There is no new stacked-container geometry or physical stability qualification.

Explicit background-only contracts
Scene jobs may declare required_visible_class_ids=[0]. Omission preserves the
original requirement for both visible classes. Taxonomy, depth/validity, native
categorical labels, instance consistency and lossless export checks still apply.
Geometry sampling reallocates absent-goods rays to structure only under this
explicit policy; it preserves the ray budget and retains equipment coverage.

Auxiliary supervision opts into missing_class_policy=
background_only_zero_foreground_mass. Mixed targets retain the original loss.
Background-only targets use (1-f)*mean(background cross-entropy), where
f=0.14835623268065162 is fixed by real training masks. Missing foreground mass
is zero, not redistributed. Ignored targets have zero gradient. Foreground-only,
all-ignored, illegal-class and nonfinite cases are rejected. This changes the
overall auxiliary foreground exposure, so previous controls cannot be reused.

Matched comparison prepared
Both new arms retain every primary real input 15 times: 450 presentations.
Each adds 60 auxiliary inputs with 30 background-only and 30 mixed donors.
The real-replay arm uses the 30 real training references; the scene arm uses the
six admitted paired renders. Deterministic cycles balance visits within each
presence group without consuming the primary shuffle/model RNG. The executed
presence counts are recorded and checked by the comparison, not merely declared.
Both arms use seeds 17/29/43, the same initialization, 120 optimizer updates,
auxiliary weight 0.2, fixed final checkpoint, resolution and optimizer settings.
Mean IoU must increase with no mean precision or recall regression. Report native
frame-weighted scores, background false positives and dark-goods misses separately.
Background-only frame recall remains undefined. No automatic model promotion.

Verification and current gates
The full suite passed 256 tests and 168 subtests. A known-plane fixture exercises
negative export, admission, actual training and strict checkpoint reload for all
three seeds in both arms. These are fixture runs, not customer-model results.
Automated visual assessment covers all six native RGB/overlay pairs; assessor
provenance remains recorded in the source metadata.
All categorical pixels agree with declared instance roles. Independent checking
of 1920 rays, including 384 targeted pallet rays, gives exact instance agreement;
maximum per-view p95 axial residual is below 0.000055 m. This is geometric
correspondence, not physical accuracy. Raw, unqualified exports remain blocked.

All six exact exports now have explicit appearance/label acceptance and passed
source-bound admission against the required 128-artifact reference cohort.
Both new three-seed arms completed with the frozen negative/mixed schedules.
Mean real-only versus real+synthetic IoU is 51.63% versus 51.42%; precision is
55.86% versus 54.40%; recall is 87.42% versus 90.40%. The candidate fails the
declared utility rule and is not promoted. All 72 native masks were recounted.
No held-out test evaluation occurred. See docs/equipment_pair_results.txt.
