Dense warehouse cargo arrangement comparison
===========================================

The second and final bounded candidate failed the frozen utility gates. This two-candidate campaign is closed; reconsider the synthesis approach before another campaign.
No model was promoted and the locked test was not evaluated.

The exact eight-capture preview received explicit user appearance/label acceptance, including the previously disclosed small empty-RGB byte exception. Admission and the protocol were frozen before two fresh three-seed arms. The source export and preview remain immutable.

Intervention: positions of 60 existing carton/tyre instances only. Counts, IDs, dimensions, mesh/material/UV bindings, original cargo/equipment, cameras, lights and render settings were preserved. Four loaded images changed; four empty control scenes were fixed. Local bay coverage increased in all four loaded views, while whole-image goods pixels increased in three and decreased slightly in one.

Limits
- One repeatedly used development trajectory; dependent frames and three seeds do not establish independent generalization or statistical significance.
- Scores measure labelled visible pixels, not the number of complete cartons or tyres recognised.
- Real reference labels remain human-reviewed model-assisted, not independent ground truth.
- Same four loaded synthetic views, repeated auxiliary inputs, opaque assets and one aisle family; transparent wrapping and sensor fidelity unsupported.
- Arrangement also changes visible area and occlusion; pixel exposure does not measure gradients or prove a training cause.
- Strict empty RGB byte equality remains failed; at most one intensity level differs on fewer than 0.014% of pixels. Depth and categorical controls are byte-exact.
- No image score or aggregate improvement establishes navigation, physical stability, calibrated sensor accuracy or the separate simulated-LiDAR requirement.

Decision details, exact gates, all seeds, frame changes, figures and exposure evidence remain in the versioned local result snapshot.
