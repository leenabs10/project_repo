Wrapping representation feasibility: proposed requirements
=========================================================

Current status
Unsupported. The implemented scene contract and independent nearest-hit checks
assume static opaque surfaces. No transparency, loose-film rendering or sensor
response has been qualified. This document declares an investigation boundary;
it does not enable a renderer feature or change existing goods labels.

Separate two cases
Opaque outer packaging around an identifiable stored load may be a goods surface
under the existing task semantics. Detached loose film, empty wrapping and film
on an empty handling container do not establish goods. Unknown contents remain
unknown. Transparent film needs its own visibility/depth contract; neither a
solid transparent-looking box nor a bright texture qualifies that case.

Questions to settle before implementation
1. Define the label rule for identifiable goods seen through film, film extending
   beyond the load, and ambiguous reflective/opaque regions. Keep unresolved
   annotation regions ignored (255); never fill hidden goods surfaces. Record
   an explicit task revision if these decisions change existing task semantics.
2. Distinguish rendered geometric surface depth from the task-visible semantic
   surface. A transparent ray may encounter film before identifiable goods.
   Define both surfaces explicitly if needed. Do not call either one measured
   RGB-D response without source-bound sensor evidence. Axial Z stays in metres.
3. Specify transmission, film thickness, roughness, refractive index and layered
   geometry in a versioned portable contract. Declare authored versus measured
   parameters and supported backend behavior. Reject unknown fields/backends;
   the current opaque contract continues to reject transparency requests.
4. Define instance ownership through film and around film edges. Exclude rack,
   pallet and handling-container surfaces even when wrapped. Do not use renderer
   object membership alone as proof of a correct goods target.

Minimum fixture matrix
- A visible opaque carton: unchanged baseline semantics and axial depth.
- The same carton with one film layer: declared goods/film surface separation.
- An empty wrapped handling container: no inferred foreground contents.
- Detached loose film beside goods: explicit ownership and ignored regions.
- Film over rack/pallet boundaries: visible support stays background.
- Two film layers, folds, holes and grazing views: bounded supported behavior,
  with rejection where the material/visibility rule is not implemented.
- Opaque or highly reflective portions hiding the load: no hidden target fill.

Required evidence
Compare RGB, visible semantic IDs, instance ownership, axial depth and validity
at native resolution on every fixture. Use independent geometric checks for
the declared surfaces; the existing opaque first-hit test cannot certify
transmission. Preserve categorical export round trips and task-version bindings.
After technical checks, require explicit exact-batch human appearance/label
acceptance. Rejected outputs remain quarantined.

Decision rule
Keep wrapping outside admitted training data until the semantics and fixtures
pass. First test whether opaque wrapped loads can be represented honestly with
the existing backend; transparent loose film may require new contract/worker
work. A backend change is justified only by a demonstrated fidelity need and
still requires full qualification. No timetable or utility gain is established
by this feasibility specification.
