Warehouse scene generation

Current supported-scene experiment
The revised tyre/carton batch is explicitly accepted, admitted and evaluated.
Mean goods IoU decreases from 49.66% for matched real replay to 48.23% with scenes;
precision and recall also decrease. The candidate fails the declared utility rule.
Scene expansion is paused; the retained model and locked test remain unchanged.
See docs/scene_benchmark_results.txt for native recounts, seed/frame outcomes and
remaining limits. The earlier accepted-batch and replacement-experiment sections
below remain separate historical evidence.

Contact refinement
A separate revision corrects nine carton positions while preserving tyre geometry,
camera/material settings and class roles. Its eight paired exports and the exact
frozen reference cohort pass independent checks. This revision has its own explicit
acceptance and frozen source/admission binding. Its completed experiment fails the
utility rule. See docs/scene_contact_review.txt and docs/scene_benchmark_results.txt.

Portable interface
Resolved intent separates metric geometry, camera poses, materials, sensor
conventions and training-only conditioning from executable paths and storage roots.
The isolated backend is blender_cycles. Scene 1.0 supports static opaque cuboids;
scene 1.1 additionally supports registered normalized triangle assets and UV maps.
Goods semantics remain in the task adapter, outside the dataset-independent core.

Source-backed appearance coverage
Eight native 1280x720 captures cover empty equipment, a pallet carton, a sealed bag,
mixed packaging, crate occlusion, near/far objects and offset aisle views. Rack
occupancy is resolved asymmetrically with a fixed seed. Footplates, original floor
and wood materials, camera offsets and bounded light differences add scene detail.
The configuration is configs/warehouse_goods_quality.json; the resolver is
src.synthesis.warehouse_quality. The older four-view mesh pilot remains preserved.

Three source meshes provide distinct carton, sealed cement-bag and open lattice-
crate geometry. Original 4K color/roughness maps retain source UVs. Uniform scaling
preserves source proportions. Front/back orientations are derivatives of each
source, not six independent assets. The bag is an exploratory packaging case;
it is not a verified TorWIC inventory item or an inferred customer requirement.
All cases are authored; the aisle is not a reconstruction of the customer warehouse.

The source files match publisher byte sizes/MD5 values and local SHA256 bindings.
The asset catalog retains source/license URLs, conversion details and derivatives.
The restricted glTF converter accepts one opaque, untransformed mesh primitive,
checks accessor ranges and array budgets, rotates Y-up to Z-up and flips UV V.
Unsupported transparency, transforms, extensions and sparse/interleaved data fail.
No source scripts, normal maps or displacement maps are applied. The renderer
computes mesh normals. Conversion and execution remain separate adapters.

Geometry and target integrity
RGB, axial depth, validity, semantic and instance outputs share one scene/camera.
A known 10 m plane establishes axial depth. Native arrays and categorical PNG
round-trips pass. Empty pallets and handling containers remain background; their
identifiable loads remain goods. Crate openings preserve the actual visible surface
behind them rather than receiving filled rectangular masks.

The independent checker src.validation.scene_quality balances goods mesh, equipment
mesh and structure rays. All 2,048 instance hits match; per-view p95 depth error is
below 0.00005 m. An additional 16 rays verify visible loads through crate openings.
These establish geometric correspondence, not measured sensor accuracy. Foreground
loads retain 590 to 22,979 native visible pixels. All 128 real-reference artifacts
retain their hashes. Pallet footprints, separate load overlap and cross-role
penetration are checked before rendering. Bounding boxes do not certify stability.

Software and experiment compatibility
The complete current suite passes 230 tests and 112 subtests. Default pytest
collection targets tests/, excluding frozen implementation copies inside reports.
Fixtures cover conversion, units/UVs, disk budgets, support/collisions, source split,
balanced rays, admission, paired targets, checkpoint declarations and comparison.

Active scene benchmark configs explicitly declare checkpoint_policy=final_epoch
and selection_metric=null. Validation and comparison recognize the exact legacy
validation-IoU metadata on previously frozen final-epoch controls as redundant.
The effective selection policy remains final epoch. Contradictory declarations fail.
No saved control configuration, checkpoint, prediction or metric is rewritten.
The source-bound control snapshot verifies unchanged and can be reused; this
metadata clarification does not require retraining.

Dataset eligibility and utility
All eight captures have source-bound appearance/label acceptance for the bounded
experiment. Original exports remain quarantined; the admitted snapshot is separate.
src.validation.scene_admission verifies exact dataset/job hashes, independent
geometry, task/cohort versions and split isolation before creating a separate
eligible snapshot. Pending decisions and stale evidence fail. The original source
export and all prior reports remain intact.

src.training.scene_inputs verifies admission and selects RGB with its own rendered
visible target. It never attaches a real source mask to a newly generated scene.
The fixed-final comparison uses seeds 17/29/43, 15 epochs, batch size 4, identical
initialization and 120 updates/450 presentations per seed. Scene replacement uses
20% of training slots; evaluation inputs and targets remain real and unchanged.
Mean IoU must improve without mean precision or recall regression. Independent
native prediction recounts are required; model promotion is never automatic.
The customer scene arm and utility comparison are complete. Mean IoU decreases
48.63% -> 48.50% and recall decreases 89.76% -> 82.71%; the candidate is rejected.
All 72 native development predictions independently reproduce saved scores. The
retained model is unchanged. Held-out evaluation has not run. Detailed outcomes
and distribution observations are in docs/scene_benchmark_results.txt.

Qualification limits
Appearance acceptance covers this bounded batch. One authored aisle layout is reused. Three meshes do not establish broad diversity,
photographic whole-scene fidelity or customer-domain coverage. Image statistics are
diagnostics, not appearance gates or a calibrated camera response. No physical
stability, moving scans, simulated LiDAR, calibrated noise or remote execution is
implemented. BlenderProc remains unavailable pending its separate dependencies.
Additional backends, including Isaac, are selected when requirements justify them
and require their own validated asset/material/sensor conversion and fixtures.
