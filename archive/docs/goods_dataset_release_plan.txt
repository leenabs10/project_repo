Warehouse goods dataset release qualification
============================================

Release objective
Qualify a versioned synthetic dataset for visible warehouse goods segmentation.
Demonstrate at least three percentage points mean goods IoU improvement under
a matched training budget, preserving precision and recall on qualified real
evaluation references. This is a task-specific release, not universal realism,
navigation qualification or completion of the same-scene LiDAR requirement.

Current campaign result (2026-11-04)
The second and final bounded candidate failed the frozen utility gates. This two-candidate campaign is closed; reconsider the synthesis approach before another campaign.
Both allowed candidate revisions have been used. The first grouped-cargo experiment remains rejected. The second tested cargo positions with the same object inventory and settings, after exact-batch review and source-bound admission. Both fresh comparison arms used the frozen protocol and three seeds. The RGB byte-control exception remains recorded.
See docs/dense_layout_coverage_results.txt for the final candidate result and docs/grouped_cargo_coverage_results.txt for the first candidate. All acceptance criteria below remain unchanged.

Source and reference boundary
Use the explicitly selected corrected cohort of 32 training and 12 development
references, profile SHA256

Earlier 30/12 cohorts, targets, models and decisions remain preserved. References
are human-reviewed model-assisted annotations, with their original provenance
and uncertainty masks. Structural checks are not independent semantic accuracy.
Derive generation parameters and coverage requirements from training sources only.
The locked test trajectory remains excluded from development and model selection.

Gate A: reproducible measurement
Verify reference/task/configuration/initialization/code bindings and record the
complete runtime policy. Repeat seed 17 in fresh processes using the same inputs
and ordered schedule. Compare model tensors, loss histories, counts and native
predictions; file-container hashes alone do not establish tensor equality.
Investigate material differences before claiming a small improvement. If an
explicit numerical implementation or runtime policy is introduced, bind it to
a new protocol and run both comparison arms under that policy. Do not reinterpret
historical candidates or replace their controls. No package upgrade is implied.

Gate B: declared coverage
Inventory the complete selected training cohort and the admitted synthetic batch
at native 1280x720 and training 640x360 geometry. RGB resampling is BOX; categorical
targets use NEAREST. Report ignore masks, foreground and background counts,
display-luminance strata, connected-region areas and executed input exposure.
Connected regions are not objects; display darkness is not calibrated radiance.
Human review is required for object-family, wrapping, placement and occlusion tags.
Required categories include training-supported load families, apparent sizes,
occupied-rack and pallet context, partial occlusion, lighting and equipment negatives.
Record distinct source assets/layouts and prevent repeated views from implying
independent diversity. Set per-category quotas after the source inventory and
before rendering. Until those quotas and semantic tags exist, coverage is pending.

Gate C: focused candidate qualification
Use the measured inventory and training-source appearance review to select one
primary representation gap. Large grouped/wrapped loads are a current review
priority, not a proven cause of model errors. Prepare a bounded pilot before
expansion. Preserve source asset proportions, licenses, metric geometry, visible
labels, ignored real targets, clean references and original accepted batches.
Check depth, support/contact, relevant occlusion and RGB/target correspondence.
Keep unsupported material/sensor requests blocked rather than approximating them.
Require explicit appearance/label decisions bound to the exact dataset/job.
Admit only a separate verified snapshot. Approval of this plan is not approval
of images that have not been rendered or reviewed.

Gate D: frozen matched development comparison
Use fresh real-only replay and hybrid arms, seeds 17/29/43, matched per-seed
initialization, optimizer updates, primary real exposure and checkpoint policy.
Freeze the source-bound schedule and all presentation counts before fitting.
Preserve the declared model, loss objective, normalization, input geometry and
fixed-final checkpoint rule. Disclose the real replay replaced by synthetic inputs,
class exposure and all numerical implementation changes. Score native real
development outputs and independently recount confusion counts after strict reload.

Acceptance contract for this campaign
- Mean goods IoU gain: at least +3.00 percentage points versus the matched control.
- Mean precision: strictly increases.
- Mean recall: does not decrease.
- Mean background false-positive rate: strictly decreases.
- Mean dark-goods miss rate: no worse than the matched control and the current
  same-reference historical ceiling of 0.18671718261675818.
- Mean frame-weighted goods IoU: does not decrease.
- IoU improves on at least two of the three paired seeds.
The frame-weighted and seed-consistency gates are additions for this campaign;
they do not change past experiment decisions. Undefined class rates remain null.
Report all seeds, frames and gains/regressions. A higher aggregate score or one
passing seed cannot override a failed guard. Three seeds and dependent frames
alone do not establish statistical significance or independent generalization.

Campaign stopping rule
Allow at most two candidate dataset revisions. Start a second revision only
when the complete first result supports a specific new hypothesis. A repeatability
diagnostic is not a candidate dataset revision. If both candidates fail, close
the campaign with its failure evidence and reconsider synthesis before expansion.
Do not change references, thresholds, gates or report subsets to recover a pass.
Model promotion and Git publication are separate decisions.

Gate E: held-out confirmation
Prepare a source-bound reference qualification and sampling protocol separately
from generation. Select evaluation membership using predefined temporal/coverage
rules, not candidate prediction errors. Prefer reference review blinded to model
outputs. Establish sufficient independent temporal groups and condition denominators
before declaring the reference sample sufficient; frame count alone is inadequate.
Final evaluation additionally requires the candidate, trained arms, numerical
policy and all acceptance rules to be frozen before locked-test access.
Require the same relative overall utility gates; the historical development
dark-miss ceiling is population-bound and cannot be copied to held-out data.
Define its held-out condition guards before evaluation. Report uncertainty and
recording dependence. Insufficient reference coverage means incomplete evidence.
A final failure closes this release attempt. Exposed test results cannot become
another tuning set; later work needs a new independent evaluation strategy.

Gate F: delivery and completion
Deliver accepted samples, checksummed manifests, labels/ignore rules, calibration,
actual annotation/assessor provenance, generation settings/seeds, quality and
coverage/rejection evidence, model comparison results, runtime versions and a
reproduction guide. Verify payload round-trips and the final code/config snapshot.
Separate package completion from utility qualification. A utility-qualified release
requires technical integrity, exact-batch acceptance, coverage, development utility,
held-out utility and reproducible packaging all to pass. Publish remaining limits.
