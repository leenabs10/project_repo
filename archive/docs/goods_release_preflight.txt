Goods dataset release preflight
===============================

Current result
The bounded repeatability gate passes under an explicit strict numerical policy.
Training-only coverage measurements are complete; semantic coverage qualification
and candidate generation remain pending. No synthetic-utility comparison, model
promotion, held-out evaluation or publication is part of this preflight.

Observed repeatability issue
Two fresh seed-17 controls using the recorded historical runtime policy produced
47.9081% and 45.5538% development goods IoU: a 2.3542 percentage-point difference.
They used matching configurations, initialization, reference files and input order.
The saved historical seed-17 control remains 43.8622%; none of these new controls
replace its evidence. The diagnostic demonstrates within-policy variation; it does
not isolate every source of historical checkpoint differences.

Strict deterministic mode rejected CUDA NLL mean reduction in the installed
PyTorch runtime. An opt-in loss setting, reduction_policy=explicit_valid_mean,
uses unreduced cross-entropy and explicit valid target-weight normalization.
Its mathematical objective and weighted gradients agree with the original loss
in focused fixtures. Floating-point reduction order can change the trajectory.
Omitting the setting retains the historical implementation exactly.

With this reduction and strict deterministic algorithms, CUBLAS workspace
:4096:8, float32, TF32 disabled, four CPU threads and cuDNN disabled, two fresh
seed-17 controls produced exactly equal values for all 360 saved model tensors,
complete loss histories and saved metrics. Both scored 43.0848% development IoU.
All 24 native development prediction masks agree across the repeats. Independent
two-dimensional confusion histograms reproduce both saved confusion totals.
Strict checkpoint reload passes. This is reproducibility evidence, not improved
model accuracy. A future experiment needs fresh matched arms under the new policy.

Hardware and measurement scope
NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5070 Laptop GPU; existing Python 3.12.15,
PyTorch 2.9.1+cu128 / torchvision 0.24.1+cu128. RGB training is 640x360;
native scoring is 1280x720; float32, batch four, 120 updates, 480 primary plus
60 auxiliary presentations per seed. Strict diagnostic training scopes took
66.18 and 64.60 seconds; peak allocated CUDA tensor memory was 1,415,031,296 bytes.
These exclude interpreter startup, inventory, rendering and independent recounts.
They are not end-to-end throughput or scalability measurements.

Training coverage findings
All 32 selected training pairs and eight admitted synthetic pairs were decoded.
Development payloads were integrity-checked but not decoded for coverage; no test
image was decoded. Native and training-resolution counts preserve ignore ID 255.
At 640x360, the real cohort contains 792,999 goods pixels in one visit per sample;
the synthetic batch contains 23,425. These totals have different sample counts
and are not directly comparable exposure budgets. Real goods occupy 15.02% of
scorable pixels, versus 1.27% in the synthetic batch. Two real frames and four
synthetic captures contain no labelled goods.

Under the actual completed 540-presentation schedule, hybrid goods pixel exposure
is 4.88% lower and dark-goods exposure is 3.46% lower than the matched control.
Those are observations, not gradient measurements or causal explanations.
Four-connected regions at training resolution include 274/214/108 real regions
in area bins 1-64 / 65-1024 / above 1024 pixels; synthetic counts are 9/9/4.
These are binary regions, not annotated instances or physical-distance estimates.

Visual triage of the training contact sheets and selected native views shows
densely grouped carton stock, wrapped surfaces and equipment occlusion alongside
the sparse authored synthetic aisle. This motivates a focused source-bound review
of grouped/wrapped-load representation. It does not certify category coverage,
physical realism or the unique cause of the previous recall regressions.

Limits
Strict repeatability is verified for seed 17 twice on one hardware/runtime setup,
not all possible systems or full multi-seed experiments. Development has been
repeatedly used and contains dependent video frames. References remain reviewed
model-assisted annotations. Qualified held-out reference coverage, final utility,
simulated LiDAR and broader customer-domain fidelity remain open.
