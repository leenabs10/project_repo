Reviewed real training coverage comparison
==========================================

Outcome
Adding the two qualified training views did not improve aggregate goods IoU,
precision or recall in the matched three-seed experiment. The new model is not
promoted. Both reviewed annotations remain qualified and preserved; unsuccessful
training is not evidence that their labels should be changed.

Goods IoU                                49.44%              48.48%   -0.96 pp
Precision                                53.75%              52.51%   -1.24 pp

The quality rule was frozen before training: mean goods IoU and precision
increase with no mean recall regression. It failed. Pixel-weighted metrics are
computed within each seed and then averaged; frame-mean IoU weights frames
equally. Ignored target ID 255 is excluded throughout. No significance claim is
made from these three seeds and dependent development images.

Five development frames improve in mean IoU, six regress and one is unchanged.
Frame 000188 has the largest gain: 48.57% to 63.43% (+14.86 pp). Frame 000094
gains 2.13 pp and dark-goods frame 000753 gains 1.22 pp. The largest regression

The two largest gains and regressions were visually inspected across all seeds.
The gallery retains every development frame and seed, including regressions.

Visible improvement in 000188 includes less false foreground around the yellow
support. Remaining errors include false goods on background equipment in 000376
and broad distant background confusion in 001036. Binary masks and these visual
comparisons do not establish a unique causal explanation for the model changes.

Qualified reference addition
The new cohort adds Aisle_CCW_Run_1/000207 and Aisle_CCW_Run_1/000383, saved with
explicit human review by Leena. Actual model-assisted annotation provenance,
source hashes, saved-submission hashes and review history are retained. This is
a separate 32-training/12-development cohort. The prior 30-training/12-development
cohort and default retained model are preserved. All original reference rows,
RGB and targets are unchanged; development membership and payloads match exactly.
Native and 640x360 training-target statistics were recalculated using training
rows only. These statistics did not change the unweighted training loss.

Matched protocol
Both new arms use plain real-only training with no auxiliary or synthetic stream.
This differs from the earlier equipment replay-control protocol. The historical
51.63% control is not used to isolate the effect of these two added references.

Model: DeepLabV3 MobileNetV3 Large, RGB, same checksum-bound pretrained weights.
Seeds: 17, 29, 43; all class heads initialized identically within each paired seed.
Loss: unweighted cross-entropy, ignore 255. AdamW: learning rate 0.0003,
weight decay 0.0001. Frozen BatchNorm running statistics. Float32, batch four,
15 epochs, 120 optimizer updates per seed, fixed final checkpoint selection.
Each real training sample appears exactly 15 times: 450 control presentations
versus 480 expanded presentations. The 6.67% exposure increase and final-batch
size difference are consequences of the added data within matched updates.

Training RGB: 640x360 BOX; categorical targets: nearest-neighbour. Evaluation:
1280x720, bilinear logits with align_corners=False, then argmax (threshold 0.5).
No threshold tuning, intermediate checkpoint selection or model promotion occurs.

Runtime and verification
The first control attempt failed at epoch six with a CUDA illegal-memory-access
error. Its artifacts and log are preserved and excluded from scores. Both arms
then restarted from the same initialization with cuDNN disabled and synchronous
CUDA launches. The completed comparison uses that execution policy in both arms;
the underlying cause of the initial CUDA failure has not been established.
Python 3.12.15, PyTorch 2.9.1+cu128 and existing packages were reused unchanged.
No training source code or package versions were changed for this experiment.

Hardware: RTX 5070 Laptop GPU, 8 GB VRAM; four CPU threads. Measured per-seed
training/reload/report scope was 33.88-36.34 seconds, excluding the subsequent
separate evaluation and prediction gallery exports. PyTorch peak allocated GPU
memory was 1,347,813,888-1,348,508,160 bytes; this is not total device memory use.
The exact benchmark runner fixture passed before execution. All six completed
checkpoints reload strictly; fixed-final evaluations match. All 72 native
development prediction masks were independently recounted. The original cohort
and retained historical checkpoints were preserved. No locked-test evaluation,
Git publication or model promotion occurred.

Interpretation
The experiment measures the effect of adding two reviewed, dependent views under
this fixed training protocol. Better annotation coverage alone did not establish
better aggregate development performance. It also does not establish that the
annotations are wrong. Preserve the reviewed data and rejected candidate.
Further work should address broader, distinct real equipment and clutter
coverage; any change to data, loss, model, resolution or training budget needs a
separate declared comparison. Independent recordings remain necessary for stronger
generalization evidence. These results do not measure synthetic-data utility.
