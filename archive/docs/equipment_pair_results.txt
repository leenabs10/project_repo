Empty/loaded equipment benchmark results
=======================================

Outcome
The accepted six-capture batch passed admission and the paired training loader.
Both new arms completed seeds 17, 29 and 43. Mean recall reaches 90.40% with
synthetic examples, but precision and goods IoU regress against the matched
real-only control. The candidate fails the declared utility rule and is not
promoted. Earlier models, references and unsuccessful experiments are preserved.

IoU, precision and recall aggregate native scorable pixels within each seed,
then average seeds. Frame-mean IoU weights frames equally and answers a different
question. Background error divides FP by scorable background pixels; incorrect
goods prediction fraction divides FP by predicted goods pixels. Accuracy is
dominated by background and does not establish useful goods segmentation.
Ignored pixels are excluded throughout. Dark uses Pillow display luma below 50;
this is not calibrated radiance or an object-family ground-truth metric.

Paired changes and seed variation
Mean IoU: -0.2180 percentage points; precision: -1.4560; recall: +2.9816.
17: IoU -2.27 pp; precision -4.16 pp; recall +4.33 pp.
29: IoU +1.57 pp; precision +2.22 pp; recall -1.25 pp.
43: IoU +0.04 pp; precision -2.43 pp; recall +5.86 pp.
Candidate recall ranges from 89.80% to 91.03%. The 90.40% result is a seed mean,
not a guarantee that every seed or frame achieves 90%. No statistical significance
or independent generalization is established by these three seeds.

Frame-level findings
Four development frames gain in mean IoU, seven regress and one is unchanged.
The largest gain is 000753, containing the dark tyre load: 29.61% to 48.53% IoU
(+18.92 pp). The largest regression is 000188: 52.94% to 46.11% (-6.83 pp).
Frame 000282 regresses by 3.97 pp; 000565 improves by 1.68 pp. The two largest
gains and regressions were visually inspected across all three seeds; the gallery
retains every development frame and seed, including all remaining regressions.

The background-only development frame 000470 improves from 22.52% to 19.80%
false-positive pixels. Its foreground recall remains undefined. This local gain
does not offset higher aggregate background false positives elsewhere. Binary
targets and intensity strata do not provide equipment-specific accuracy or prove
that one scene component caused the changes.

Controlled execution
Both arms use the same pretrained initialization, optimizer, resolution, seeds,
fixed final checkpoint and background-only loss policy. Each preserves 450
primary real presentations and adds 60 auxiliary presentations: 30 negative and
30 mixed, within 120 optimizer updates. Each synthetic capture receives ten
auxiliary presentations per seed. The real-only arm replays only training
references, including the two existing negative references. Previous controls
were not reused because their missing-class policy and exposure differ.

Training uses 640x360 float32, batch size four, on the RTX 5070 Laptop GPU.
Evaluation/prediction targets are native 1280x720. CUDA checkpoint reloads are
strict. No intermediate development score selected an earlier checkpoint.

passed 256 tests and 168 subtests, including known-fixture training/reload for

The locked test remains unevaluated. Git publication state remains unchanged.

Interpretation and next decision
The batch supplies a useful targeted signal: fewer dark-goods misses and fewer
false positives in one negative frame. It does not achieve the broader precision
goal or improve mean IoU against its matched control. Do not expand these six
scenes solely on the strength of recall or overall pixel accuracy.

Prioritize distinct, accurately reviewed real training coverage of empty handling
equipment, loaded equipment and surrounding warehouse clutter. Assess the eight
quarantined training previews for redundancy before annotation; none has been
admitted as a new target. Preserve valid existing masks. Any additional synthetic
experiment should derive its coverage and appearance from training sources,
declare its changed factor and retain the matched control. Independent real
recordings remain necessary for stronger generalization evidence.
