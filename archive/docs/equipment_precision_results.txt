Equipment/background precision experiment
========================================

The accepted batch improves mean pixel-weighted IoU and precision, but reduces
recall on every seed and fails both dark-goods preservation guards. The candidate
does not pass the frozen qualification rule and is not promoted.
Retained model/reporting baseline remains unchanged; no automatic promotion.

Metrics aggregate native scorable pixels within each seed, then average the
three seeds. Ignored target ID 255 is excluded. Frame-mean IoU weights frames
equally. Background false-positive rate divides by background pixels; precision
divides correct goods predictions by all predicted goods pixels.

Interpretation
The +3 percentage-point IoU component is met (+4.87 pp), but the complete MVP
utility milestone is not: mean recall falls by 6.77 pp and dark-goods miss rate
increases by 4.19 pp against the fresh control. It also exceeds the historical
18.54% dark-miss ceiling. Frame-mean IoU decreases, with five improving frames,
six regressing frames and one unchanged frame. The candidate reduces false goods
predictions while also missing more goods. Do not expand or promote it based only
on the aggregate IoU or precision gains. Retain the approved batch, rejected
checkpoints and every regression; no reannotation or threshold tuning follows
from this result. Any revision needs a separate frozen comparison.
