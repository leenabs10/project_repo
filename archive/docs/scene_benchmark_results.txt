These results fail the declared requirement of higher mean IoU without lower
mean precision or recall. Three seeds, related video frames and repeated use of
the development cohort do not establish statistical significance or generalization.

Protocol and executed exposure
Both arms preserve all 30 real training frames exactly 15 times: 450 primary real
presentations. Each adds 60 auxiliary inputs within 120 optimizer updates. The
control uses 60 real replay inputs, for 510 real presentations; the scene arm uses
60 accepted rendered inputs, for 450 real plus 60 rendered presentations. Every
rendered RGB input is paired with its own categorical target. Initialization,
optimizer, 640x360 training, 1280x720 evaluation, auxiliary weight 0.2 and training-
derived foreground mass 0.14835623268065162 are matched. Checkpoints are fixed at
epoch 15, with no intermediate development scoring. Strict reload checks pass.

The prior real-replay control was reused only after verifying its exact sources,
configuration, training implementation and reference cohort. The contact revision
changes nine carton Z positions; tyre geometry and all other scene settings are
preserved. Its new source/admission binding was frozen before scene training.
The earlier 450-presentation replacement experiment is a separate comparison.

Runtime
The new scene seeds took 36.70 seconds on average inside the recorded training
scope. Maximum recorded CUDA tensor allocation was 1.24 GiB. Hardware: NVIDIA
GeForce RTX 5070 Laptop GPU; float32; batch size 4. Timing excludes scene rendering
and subsequent prediction-gallery export. It is not an end-to-end throughput or
scalability benchmark.

Observed failures and limits
Largest frame-mean IoU regressions:
Aisle_CW_Run_1/000753: IoU -10.83 pp
Aisle_CW_Run_1/000094: IoU -4.18 pp
Aisle_CW_Run_1/000188: IoU -3.73 pp
Inspection across all three seeds shows persistent missed regions on the large
dark tyre load in 000753, despite adding a tyre asset. The strongest gain is
000376 (+7.24 pp). Predictions also assign goods to background structures and
equipment. Only nonignored reference pixels contribute to the reported scores;
display overlays cover the whole image. These observations do not establish a
single causal explanation or justify changing labels to raise a score.

The fixed auxiliary schedule visits every scene but is not condition-balanced:
the two pure tyre views receive 7 of 60 auxiliary inputs, mixed cargo receives 7,
and empty-equipment/partial-occlusion views receive 20. Class-mass weighting does
not guarantee balanced object-family exposure or realistic appearance. One aisle,
repeated source damage, uniform lighting, opaque materials and authored camera
placement retain a substantial domain gap. Physical sensor fidelity is unverified.

Decision and next investigation
Retain existing references, the control and all candidate artifacts. Pause scene
expansion and additional customer-model runs. Before another declared experiment,
use training-only evidence to audit object-family exposure, empty-equipment
negatives, foreground scale, occlusion and material/lighting coverage. Broader
claims require additional scene-independent qualified real evaluation coverage.
Reannotation is warranted only for demonstrated label errors. Any changed cohort,
sampler, generator or loss requires a new binding and matched comparison; do not
select favourable subsets or alter this completed result after seeing its scores.

Earlier slot-replacement experiment
===================================

Outcome
The eight appearance/label-qualified scenes passed admission and native paired
loader checks. The matched three-seed experiment completed. Its candidate is
rejected because mean IoU decreased and recall regressed on all three seeds.
The retained model and all real references remain unchanged. No locked-test
evaluation or automatic model promotion occurred.

IoU, precision and recall first aggregate native scorable pixels within each seed,

The experiment fails the predeclared mean-IoU/precision/recall nonregression rule.
Three seeds and dependent frames do not establish statistical significance or
unbiased generalization. Historical RGB-condition scores use a different protocol.

Protocol and execution
Both arms use the same generic initialization, model, optimizer, 640x360 training,
native 1280x720 evaluation, 30 real training references and 12 real development
references. Per seed: 15 epochs, batch size 4, 120 optimizer updates and 450
presentations. The hybrid schedule replaces 94 slots with scene inputs, leaving
356 real presentations. All eight accepted scenes are exercised. RGB and its own
rendered visible target are replaced together. Evaluation inputs/targets remain
real. Checkpoints are fixed at the final epoch; earlier development scoring is
disabled. Saved checkpoints pass strict reload and independent native recounts.
The completed real-only control is reused without modifying its saved artifacts.

Distribution observations
At actual training resolution and executed presentation counts, goods occupy
14.84% of supervised pixels in the control versus 12.24% in the hybrid arm.
Ignored-pixel coverage changes from 29.08% to 23.18%. The hybrid uses fewer real
presentations and more fully labelled rendered background. Presentation-weighted
mean luma changes from 128.72 to 124.49 (8-bit display values).
These measured differences and the visual domain gap are plausible contributors;
the experiment does not isolate their causal effects.

The largest frame-mean IoU regression is Aisle_CW_Run_1/000753 (-22.53 pp).
Visual inspection shows reduced detection of the large dark tire load. The current
synthetic batch contains cartons and sealed bags, with no tire-like load asset.
This is a coverage limitation and prediction error, not evidence that the saved
reference requires reannotation. Nearby frames and repeated development use limit
the independence of this diagnosis.

Next coverage requirements
Prioritize training-supported goods diversity, dark rounded/irregular loads,
foreground size and occlusion coverage, warehouse material/light variation and
equipment negatives. Match target coverage and record real exposure when designing
the next bounded protocol. Freeze its configuration before running it; do not alter
this completed experiment or select post-hoc subsets to report a gain. Additional
repeated development trials increase selection bias and must be disclosed.
Appearance qualification, physical sensor fidelity and downstream task utility
remain separate conclusions. Current acceptance is limited to the bounded batch.
