Training coverage refresh
One visit to all 32 frames at 640x360 contains 800,937 goods pixels, 4,604,145
background pixels and 1,967,718 ignored pixels. Goods occupy 14.8182% of scorable
training pixels. Dark display-luminance goods pixels number 80,924.
Native labels remain uint8 IDs 0/1/255. RGB uses BOX and targets use NEAREST.
Development references were integrity-checked but no development images were
decoded to derive these training coverage statistics. No test payload was used.
The auxiliary class-mass coefficient is recalculated from these training targets;
the previous reference-dependent value is not silently reused.

Fresh real-only control
The complete seed-17/29/43 arm uses DeepLabV3 MobileNetV3-Large with the same
checksum-bound generic initialization. Each seed uses 15 epochs, 120 updates,
480 primary real and 60 real replay auxiliary inputs, batch four and float32.
Threshold is 0.5 and checkpoints are fixed to final epoch. Strict reload and
separate evaluation pass. Native masks are exported without editing predictions.
Strict deterministic algorithms, explicit_valid_mean loss reduction, CUBLAS
workspace :4096:8, TF32 disabled, cuDNN disabled and four CPU threads are used.
This is a new numerical/reference protocol, not a direct historical improvement
comparison. It does not replace the retained reporting model.

below 50 on reference goods, not a calibrated radiance or a tyre annotation.

metrics and aggregate confusion counts using two-dimensional histograms.

Grouped-cargo candidate result
The approved eight-capture opaque grouped-cargo batch passed independent geometry
checks and was admitted with the disclosed empty-RGB byte-match failure preserved.
Its matched three-seed arm scored 48.42% IoU, 55.88% precision and 78.99% recall,
versus 50.16%, 54.20% and 87.08% for real-only replay. Dark-goods miss rate rose
from 36.48% to 54.88%; frame-mean IoU fell from 42.60% to 39.05%. The candidate
failed the frozen gates and was not promoted. Details and every seed are in
docs/grouped_cargo_coverage_results.txt and docs/goods_corrected_reference_control.json.

A second candidate requires a specific training-supported hypothesis from this
result. Transparent wrapping remains outside the supported scene contract.
