Occupied-rack cargo placement preparation
========================================

This is the preserved preparation snapshot. Exact-batch approval, admission and
the completed comparison are documented in docs/occupied_rack_context_results.txt.

Eight captures form four empty/loaded pairs. This batch tests whether occupied
rack context, smaller visible goods and partial rack occlusion address the prior
recall regression. At this preparation snapshot it was technically qualified and
awaiting explicit appearance and label acceptance. Admission, training and utility
evaluation subsequently completed; use the results document for the final decision.

Only the centre coordinates of eight existing cargo instances changed. Assets,
dimensions, orientation, materials, camera, lights, equipment and capture counts
are preserved. Placement support comes only from reviewed training frames
Aisle_CCW_Run_1/000207, 000383 and 000283. An initial fully hidden placement was
rejected before rendering; its configuration and reason remain preserved.

Qualification
All 3,040 independent nearest-surface rays agree with exported instance labels.
The largest per-capture depth p95 residual is 0.00003837 m. Each moved object has
174-1,544 visible native pixels (44-390 at training resolution), with independent
visible and rack-occluded ray evidence. Shelf contact, cargo separation, empty
equipment labels and container openings pass the sampled geometry checks.

Empty semantic, instance and depth payloads are byte exact. Repeated empty RGB
renders differ by at most 1/255 in approximately 0.004% of channels; the scene and
render settings are unchanged. Loaded RGB and labels change together with cargo
placement. Static support checks do not establish physical stability.

Exposure tradeoff
Across one visit to each capture, visible goods pixels at 640x360 fall from
54,698 to 23,425 (57.2% less); dark-goods pixels fall from 15,777 to 5,058 (67.9%
less). Darkness here means Pillow BOX-resampled display luminance below 50 on
NEAREST-resampled goods labels. It is not a calibrated sensor measurement.
The full ordered schedule is measured separately. Equal capture/visit counts do
not imply equal goods-pixel exposure or equal gradients under class-normalized
loss. This tradeoff must remain visible when assessing recall.

Per seed, the 60 scheduled synthetic visits reduce auxiliary goods pixels from
400,549 to 173,773, and dark-goods pixels from 110,459 to 35,420. Including the
unchanged 480 primary real inputs, total dark-goods pixels are 1,298,849 before,
1,223,810 after, and 1,267,616 in the real-only control. The revision therefore
has fewer dark-goods pixel presentations than the control. Foreground class-mean
loss coefficients stay fixed; these counts do not predict recall or gradients.

Frozen comparison preparation
Both arms use the corrected 32-training/12-development reference cohort. An
explicit auxiliary update sequence preserves the previous semantic donor order
despite changed job IDs. Per seed: 480 primary real presentations, 60 auxiliary
inputs and 120 optimizer updates. The candidate retains 14 tyre-loaded, 16
carton-loaded and 30 empty presentations. The control uses 60 real replays.
Thus the candidate has 60 fewer real replays than the control; total input and
update counts match. The sampler change is validated before fitting.

Seeds 17/29/43, model, initialization, optimizer, loss, auxiliary weight 0.2,
normalization, final epoch 15 and native prediction policy remain fixed. Required
gates remain at least +3 percentage points mean goods IoU, increased precision,
nondecreasing recall and lower background false-positive rate. Dark-goods miss
rate must not exceed the fresh control or the reference-corrected historical
ceiling of 18.67171826%. No automatic model promotion is allowed.

Limits and next decision
The sparse authored aisle and repeated materials do not reproduce the density,
blur or clutter of the real training recordings. This is a bounded spatial
coverage experiment, not a photographic-realism claim. Exact-batch appearance
and label acceptance, then source-bound admission, are required before both
fresh three-seed arms run. Original references, retained weights, historical
batches and unrelated model trials are preserved. The locked test is untouched.
