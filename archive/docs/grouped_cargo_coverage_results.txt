Paired goods IoU improved for seed 17 only. Seeds 29 and 43 regressed. Across
the twelve development frames, three gained, seven regressed and two were
unchanged. All 72 saved native predictions were independently recounted.

The candidate used 480 primary real presentations and 60 synthetic auxiliary
presentations per seed, within the same 120 optimizer updates and total 540
presentations as the control. The control used 60 real auxiliary replays. At
640x360, the executed control replay targets contained 800,937 goods pixels and
2,267,906 ignored pixels. The candidate targets contained 508,108 goods pixels
and no ignored pixels: 36.6% fewer goods pixels. The candidate did contain
90,783 dark goods pixels versus 80,924 in the control schedule. These counts
measure pixel exposure, not loss or gradient influence. More dark-pixel exposure
coincided with worse dark-goods recall; the comparison does not isolate a cause.

Batch qualification and exception
Eight captures were admitted after explicit user approval. Independent checks
matched 2,048 rays to rendered instance IDs at 100% agreement; the largest
capture's axial-depth p95 error was 0.000048 m. All four unchanged empty scenes
had exact depth, depth-validity and categorical label arrays. Their RGB files
did not meet the frozen byte-exact gate: each differed from the source at a very
small number of pixels by at most one intensity level. This was disclosed before
the user's approval and remains a recorded failed gate; approval did not rewrite
that measurement.

Training used seeds 17/29/43, fixed final-epoch selection, matched initialization,
strict deterministic execution and identical real references. Development
results are dependent video frames and have been used repeatedly. The approved
references remain human-reviewed and model-assisted, not independent ground
truth. No model was promoted and no test data was opened.

Next work
Preserve this rejection. Any second candidate should test a specific hypothesis
derived from the executed exposure and per-frame errors, with design support
drawn from training references only. The measured goods-pixel shortfall and
losses on frames 000753, 000282, 000376 and 000188 are useful diagnostic leads,
not proof of a cause. Keep the frozen schedule, references and acceptance gates
unless a new protocol is explicitly versioned before training. Transparent
wrapping remains unsupported; do not simulate it with opaque materials.
