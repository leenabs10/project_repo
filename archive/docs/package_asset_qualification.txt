Warehouse package asset qualification
====================================

Status
A separate six-capture quality scout has completed native-array and independent
geometry checks. Exact-batch appearance and visible-label review is pending.
The scout is not admitted for training and is not a third utility candidate.
The preceding two-candidate campaign remains closed. No model was trained,
promoted or replaced, and the existing real references remain unchanged.

Source screening
Three small source archives and their individual publisher pages were retained
with byte counts and SHA256 receipts. The two displayed source pages identify
their assets as CC0. Their authors and original source bindings are preserved.

- Yughues, Cardboards Pack:
  https://opengameart.org/content/cardboards-pack
  One additional closed-carton mesh is a goods candidate in the authored scout.
  The source-declared FBX extents are approximately 0.817 x 0.766 x 0.500 metres.
- Jhan Gutierrez, Card board box:
  https://opengameart.org/content/card-board-box
  Native inspection shows an open empty carton. It is background handling
  equipment, including in the loaded scenes. Its file imports with a maximum
  extent of 6.63 metres; a declared uniform 0.1 derivative gives an approximately
  0.635 x 0.663 x 0.300 metre preview. These authored dimensions are not an
  independently measured warehouse item.
- Yethiel, Cardboard Box:
  https://opengameart.org/content/cardboard-box
  The archive contains an opened/damaged box. It was not counted as an additional
  closed-goods design and was not used as goods in the qualified scout.

Other screened candidates were excluded when they repeated the existing source,
were stylistically unsuitable, had unavailable downloads, or had conflicting
licence/ML-use terms. Their reasons remain in the local source-screening record.
The original asset library and earlier datasets are preserved separately.

Conversion and semantic checks
The pinned Blender 4.5.3 FBX importer ran in a separate background process with
automatic script execution disabled. Downloaded .blend files and source scripts
were not loaded. FBX texture loading was replaced in that isolated process by
placeholders; explicitly selected local image files were bound separately.

Object transforms were baked into world-space triangles, split UV corners were
preserved, and source unit declarations were recorded. Both converted surfaces
have finite positive bounds, valid UVs, nondegenerate triangles and manifold
edges. Normalized NPZ meshes pass the existing contained-path/checksum/dtype
loader. RGB/TGA-to-PNG conversion preserves the decoded colour pixels.

Manifold topology does not establish that a container has a closed lid or holds
goods. An initial scout incorrectly assigned the open Jhan carton to goods.
It was stopped and quarantined before appearance qualification, admission or
training. The separately versioned corrected scout assigns the visible empty
container to background. The initial scene, logs and rejection evidence remain
preserved rather than being rewritten as successful evidence.

The supplied maps do not establish physical roughness. Both new assets use an
explicitly authored constant roughness of 0.8. Source specular maps were not
silently interpreted as roughness, and source normal maps were not applied.
These are declared material limitations requiring appearance review.

Qualified technical scope
The corrected scout contains close-carton, close-tyre and mixed-stock views,
each paired with equipment-only context. RGB, axial depth, validity, semantic
and instance payloads are native 1280x720 exports from the same resolved scene.
All six captures pass structural and categorical export checks. All 1,536
independent sampled instance hits agree; the maximum per-view axial-depth p95
residual is approximately 0.000043 metres.

All three equipment-only captures have zero goods pixels. The open carton has
31,142 or 33,954 visible instance pixels in its paired contexts; every one is
background. New closed-carton instances have 61,066 visible goods pixels in the
close-carton view and 43,713 in the mixed-stock view. These counts establish
declared visible-label correspondence, not independent annotation accuracy or
photographic realism.

Remaining qualification
The trial library adds one closed-goods carton source and one empty-container
source. Tall-carton variety, transparent wrapping, material fidelity and broader
warehouse appearance remain incomplete. No batch-volume, realism, physical
stability, calibrated sensor, LiDAR or utility claim follows from these checks.
Human acceptance must identify the exact new batch; earlier approvals do not
qualify it. A broader collection and any subsequent utility campaign require
their own coverage, admission, frozen comparison and stopping rules.
