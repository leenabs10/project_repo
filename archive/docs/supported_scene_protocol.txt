Training-supported synthesis and preserved-exposure protocol

Purpose
Improve object coverage, protect real-data exposure and isolate synthetic utility
from additional training computation. The completed replacement experiment remains
preserved and rejected. Its metrics are not reused as the new matched control.

Training-only object support
All 30 qualified training references were inspected, plus 72 uniformly spaced RGB
sources from the training trajectory. No development/test sensor payload was used
to derive this coverage. Native training frames 000223 and 000239 show stacked
tyres as palletized cargo; qualified training references also show close stacked
cartons and wrapped loads. The visual inventory is a coverage assessment with
source hashes, not new independently validated segmentation ground truth.
Wrapped loads remain a documented gap: transparent wrapping is unsupported and
is not silently approximated with an opaque box or inferred labels.

Generated coverage and appearance
Eight new native views cover close carton columns, palletized tyre stacks,
camera offsets, mixed cargo, partial occlusion, distant loads and empty equipment.
The registered tyre retains source tread geometry and original 4K color/roughness
maps. A proper metric rotation places the tyre flat; uniform scaling preserves
shape. Carton geometry, floor/wood maps and equipment labels remain source-bound.
Exposure, lower camera viewpoints and rack colors are authored training-informed
variations; no calibrated camera response or reconstructed warehouse is claimed.

The initial pre-contact export averages 12.27% foreground image coverage versus
10.54% across the
30 qualified real training references. Both low/high coverage cases pass the
training-derived checks. Mean display luma is 121.36 on an 8-bit scale. These
statistics do not prove natural appearance. One authored aisle, repeated source
assets, no normal/displacement maps, no transparent wrapping and no physical
stability simulation remain limitations. Empty equipment stays background;
identifiable visible cargo stays goods. No hidden surfaces are inferred.

All 2,048 independent instance rays match, including 1,280 targeted mesh rays.
Per-view p95 axial-depth error is below 0.00004 m. This is geometric correspondence,
not physical sensor accuracy. Native images, masks and depth/validity arrays pass
structural and lossless export checks. All 128 real-reference artifacts are unchanged.

Real exposure and foreground balance
Every original real input/target remains in the primary stream: 30 frames x 15
epochs = 450 primary real presentations per seed. The new mode does not replace
any real slot. One auxiliary input is processed every second optimizer update:
60 auxiliary presentations within the same 120 updates, or 510 total per seed.
The real-replay control receives real training donors; the scene arm receives
only admitted rendered RGB with its own visible target. Both use the same schedule,
loss weight 0.2, model, initialization, resolution, optimizer and checkpoint policy.
Extra computation is matched between the two new arms. It differs from the old
450-presentation replacement experiment, so their scores are contextual only.

Auxiliary foreground/background losses are averaged separately, then weighted
using the exact real-training scorable foreground fraction 0.14835623268065162 at
640x360. This prevents a large rendered background from changing the auxiliary
class mass solely through pixel count. It does not guarantee a particular gradient
norm, raw foreground exposure or model score. Stored labels are never modified.
The original primary real cross-entropy remains unchanged. Auxiliary donors need
both scorable classes; original negative frames remain in the primary stream.
Sampling is training-only and hash-deterministic, without changing the primary
source-slot order or consuming its generator. Losses/gradients must remain finite.

Frozen reevaluation
Seeds: 17, 29, 43. Fixed final checkpoint after 15 epochs; no intermediate
development prediction selects the checkpoint. Native 1280x720 scoring uses the
same 12 real development references. Compare the scene arm against the new real-
replay arm, with matched 450 primary real + 60 auxiliary presentations and 120
updates. Independently recount every native prediction and report per-seed and
frame-weighted results, precision and recall. Mean goods IoU must improve without
mean precision or recall regression. Report all outcomes and retain rejected runs.
No model promotion is automatic, and the locked test remains disabled.

Completed failure analysis
The source-bound audit measures executed foreground/intensity exposure, visible
rendered families and native error strata. Real references and model checkpoints
are unchanged. The analysis reproduces exactly; its verification passed 240 tests
and 130 subtests. The completed sampling extension passes 244 tests/143 subtests.
See docs/goods_failure_analysis.txt for measured findings and the proposed
source-preserving donor-schedule ablation. No further training has run.

Completed donor-sampling contrast
The optional balanced cycle policy visits every admitted scene 7 or 8 times per
60 auxiliary inputs. Exact source payloads, loss, primary real exposure and update
budgets are unchanged. The three-seed candidate reaches 49.38% IoU, below replay
at 49.66%, and fails the declared rule. Its recall is 87.77% versus 88.80%.
The retained model is unchanged. See docs/sampling_benchmark_results.txt and
