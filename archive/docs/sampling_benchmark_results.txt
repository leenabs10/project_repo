Goods donor-sampling benchmark
=============================

Outcome
The balanced scene-sampling candidate completes all three seeds and fails the
predeclared utility rule. Mean IoU improves by 1.15 percentage points over the
original scene schedule, but remains 0.29 points below matched real replay.
Recall remains 1.03 points below replay. The retained model is unchanged and
the candidate is not promoted. The locked test set remains unevaluated.

Native development results (mean of seeds 17, 29, 43)

Precision                 53.0236%    51.5184%    53.0162%

IoU, precision and recall aggregate native scorable pixels within each seed,
then average seeds. Frame-mean IoU instead weights frames equally. All original
arms and unfavourable results are retained. Precision differs from replay by only
-0.0074 percentage points; two-decimal display rounding hides that difference.
The unrounded mean metrics are used by the declared rule. No statistical
significance is implied by this small contrast.

Paired changes against real replay
17: IoU -2.28 pp; precision +0.12 pp; recall -7.53 pp
29: IoU -2.71 pp; precision -0.73 pp; recall -6.75 pp
43: IoU +4.13 pp; precision +0.59 pp; recall +11.21 pp
Two seeds regress in IoU and one improves. Three development frames improve,
seven regress and two are unchanged by mean IoU. The strongest frame regression
remains 000753, containing a dark tyre load: -13.82 points against replay and
-2.98 points against the original scene schedule. No seed or frame subset is
selected to claim a synthetic-data benefit.

Intervention and preserved contracts
The optional balanced_scene_cycle policy permutes admitted scene identities by
a stable per-cycle hash and visits every donor exactly once in each complete
cycle. Every prefix has at most one visit of imbalance. In 60 auxiliary inputs,
each of the eight scenes receives 7 or 8 visits, replacing the original 3-to-13
spread. The two pure tyre views receive 15 visits instead of 7.

The scene exports, labels, geometry, lighting, reference cohort, model, generic
initialization, loss, auxiliary weight 0.2 and foreground class mass are unchanged.
Every primary real input remains in its original stream: 30 frames x 15 epochs,
450 primary real presentations, plus 60 auxiliary inputs within 120 updates per
seed. Training is 640x360 float32, batch size 4; evaluation is native 1280x720.
Final checkpoint epoch is 15, with no earlier development scoring. The sampler
consumes no Python, NumPy or Torch random state used by primary training.

Baseline reuse is explicit. The preflight compares all model/loss/optimizer/input
function syntax with the preserved implementation and verifies that disabling
the optional policy preserves the original sampler branch. Both saved baseline
schedules reproduce exactly across all three seeds. Configuration validation and
comparison accept only the declared sampling-policy difference; loss and budget
matching remain enforced. Model checkpoints pass strict reload.

Exposure and task response
Rendered auxiliary dark-goods pixel exposure rises from 6.35% to 12.46%; rendered
tyre surfaces rise from 12.45% to 25.93% of auxiliary goods pixels. These are
source-instance and display-intensity counts, not gradient contribution or real
object-family ground truth. Despite this increase, native dark-goods miss rate
rises from 40.41% to 43.01%, against 26.45% for real replay. Simply adding more
exposure to these accepted tyre renders did not resolve the real tyre failure.

This result supports retaining appearance/shape/occlusion mismatch and limited
reference coverage as open explanations. It does not identify a unique cause,
prove that all synthesis approaches fail or establish a production conclusion.
The contrast was motivated after inspecting the earlier failed experiment.
Repeated development use, related frames, thirty training references and one
authored aisle limit independent generalization evidence.

Verification
All 244 tests and 143 subtests pass. Fixtures exercise quota balance, cadence,
input-order independence, unchanged random states, the legacy golden schedule,
invalid policy/source rejection and three-seed fixed-final fixture execution.
All 72 masks in the new replay-versus-balanced comparison are independently
recounted. The original scene arm retains its 36 previously verified masks,
giving 108 unique native predictions with verified counts across the three arms.
All 128 frozen reference artifacts remain unchanged. No source annotations,
accepted scene payloads or previous experiment artifacts are edited.

Implementation
Policy: src/training/auxiliary_inputs.py.
Configuration: configs/benchmarks/warehouse_goods_balanced_sampling.json.
Execution/comparison: python -m src.pipeline.scene_benchmark --help.
The new policy is opt-in; previous configurations keep their existing behavior.
Its availability is an engineering capability, not a recommendation to use the
failed model or a claim of synthetic-data utility.

Decision and next data-quality work
Retain the replay control and current model. Do not expand this scene batch on
the strength of sampler balance. Prioritize a small training-source comparison
of real and rendered tyre silhouette, scale, material, illumination and occlusion,
then qualify representative appearance changes under a new exact batch binding.
Additional independent real reference coverage remains necessary for stronger
claims. Existing reviewed labels need changes only if a specific error is found.
The next experiment must declare its changed factor and complete reference/scene
bindings before training. No further training or new scene generation is included
in this completed contrast.
