Equipment precision experiment
==============================

Purpose
Reduce background incorrectly predicted as goods while protecting the dark-goods
benefit observed in the completed empty/loaded comparison. The change under
investigation is a source-bound synthetic equipment/load batch, not altered
real annotations, model architecture, loss, threshold or checkpoint selection.

Established reporting baseline
Retain the existing model and its revised-reference development scores:
goods IoU 50.84%, precision 54.48%, recall 88.74%, mean across seeds 17/29/43.
The scores apply to the revised native 12-frame development targets, not to the
historical targets used when its original report was created. Retained weights,
old pointers and experiment history remain preserved.

Reference cohort for the new experiment
Use the explicit expanded cohort with 32 reviewed training and 12 development
references, profile SHA256
df43b6eae5204100ccdeccebcf504761367d4ce1790769fd4e78ed14d4bcbe4d.
Both added training annotations remain qualified. The unsuccessful 32-view
experiment is not evidence that their labels are wrong. Preserve all targets.
Development membership and RGB/target payloads must agree with the historical
equipment comparison before its dark-goods diagnostic is used as a guardrail.

Candidate and execution gates
Prepare empty/loaded equipment pairs with additional visible background equipment
and both dark tyres and cartons. Camera, equipment, materials and lighting match
within each pair; only explicit cargo is added. Empty equipment is background;
only visible identifiable cargo is goods. Derived geometry, native labels, depth,
source assets and training-only support are independently checked.

Appearance/label approval binds to the exact rendered dataset and job hashes.
Passing technical checks or a general instruction to prepare an experiment does
not supply that batch-specific decision. Pending or changed outputs cannot enter
training. Exact approval and admission are recorded. The frozen comparison is
complete and rejected for recall and dark-goods regressions; see the results in
docs/equipment_precision_results.txt. Preparation snapshots retain their original
pending state and the original protocol is not rewritten after observing scores.

Prepared batch
Four matched pairs/eight native 1280x720 captures are prepared: two tyre-load
pairs and two carton-load pairs, with additional empty pallet stacks and empty
lattice containers. All empty captures have only background labels. Native goods
labels match the declared visible cargo instances exactly; added equipment is
background. Geometry checks passed 2,048 role-balanced rays plus 576 rays focused
on the added equipment. All sampled instance hits agree; native per-view geometry
p95 depth residuals are below 0.00005 metres. These are renderer-correspondence
checks, not measured sensor accuracy or physical load stability.

Explicit human appearance/label acceptance is recorded for all eight captures;
they passed admission into a separate eligible snapshot. The original export stays
unchanged and ineligible. AI visual triage inspected the eight empty RGB/loaded overlay
views; this is not independent reference annotation or a measured accuracy score.
The repeated aisle/materials and simple pallet geometry remain appearance limits.

Exposure disclosure
Both arms use 480 primary real presentations and 60 auxiliary presentations within
120 updates per seed. Control adds 60 real replays; candidate adds 60 synthetic
inputs, so the candidate has 60 fewer real replays. Each arm schedules 30 negative
and 30 mixed auxiliary inputs. The synthetic schedule gives the two tyre-loaded
captures 14 presentations combined, compared with 20 in the prior experiment.
This decrease is disclosed rather than described as preserved dark-goods exposure.
The measured dark-goods guard remains mandatory. Pixel exposure is not gradient
contribution. Primary exposure also differs from the older 30-view experiment's
450 presentations; only the new matched control isolates this candidate.

Fresh matched comparison
Use new real-only replay and real-plus-synthetic arms on the same frozen 32/12
cohort, seeds 17/29/43, exact checksum-bound pretrained initialization, matched
optimizer updates, loss and auxiliary schedule, threshold and final checkpoint
selection. Disclose primary/auxiliary presentations and class-presence exposure.
The replay arm fills auxiliary slots with real training references; it does not
receive synthetic images. Do not reuse an earlier control from another protocol.

Acceptance criteria fixed before fitting
- Positive experimental result: mean goods IoU and precision increase, mean recall
  does not decrease, and mean scorable-background false-positive rate decreases
  against the fresh matched control.
- MVP utility milestone: all of the above plus at least +3 percentage points
  mean goods IoU against that control. A smaller gain is progress, not completion.
- Dark-goods preservation: mean dark-goods miss rate does not exceed either the
  fresh control or the exact historical empty/loaded candidate diagnostic.
  Historical miss rate: 0.1853674019996826. The stratum is visible target goods
  with Pillow RGB-to-L display luminance below 50, ignored targets excluded.
  This is an intensity diagnostic, not a tyre-specific annotation or calibrated
  radiance. The historical floor is a strict preservation guard, not a matched
  causal comparison. No absolute 90.40% aggregate recall guarantee is asserted.

Report all seeds and development frames, including gains and regressions,
pixel-weighted and frame-weighted IoU, background false positives and dark-goods
misses. Preserve rejected models. A generic comparison pass does not override
these stricter gates and does not authorize automatic model promotion.

Limits
The candidate remains based on authored layouts and a small training-supported
asset library. Correct geometry/labels do not establish photorealism, calibrated
sensor fidelity or downstream usefulness. Three seeds and reused dependent
development frames do not establish independent generalization. Freeze candidate
and evaluation protocol before separate-recording assessment. Keep the locked test
reserved for final selection. No new annotation round or broader scan is required
by this experiment preparation.
