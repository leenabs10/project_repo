Occupied-rack cargo placement results
====================================

Question: does training-supported rack placement, smaller visible cargo and
partial rack occlusion improve real-image goods segmentation under the fixed
equipment/background protocol?

Fails the complete frozen utility rule. Candidate is not promoted; retained model remains the reporting baseline.

Retained reporting baseline on these corrected references: goods IoU 50.63%,
precision 54.26%, recall 88.68%. Its unchanged weights and historical selection
policy are preserved; it is not the fresh matched control in this trial.

Eight exact captures received explicit appearance/label approval from Leena
Bhise and passed source-bound admission. Appearance/label acceptance is
separate from downstream usefulness and does not certify photographic realism.

Acceptance requires at least +3 percentage points mean goods IoU, increased
precision, nondecreasing recall and lower background false-positive rate.
Dark-goods miss rate must not exceed the fresh control or the same-reference
historical ceiling of 18.67171826%. The historical guard is not a matched
causal control. No threshold, loss or acceptance rule was changed after fitting.

Each seed uses 480 primary real and 60 auxiliary presentations, 120 updates,
the same initialization/optimizer/loss and fixed final epoch 15. Both arms
use 30 mixed and 30 background-only auxiliary inputs. The candidate retains
the exact ordered 14 tyre-loaded/16 carton-loaded/30 empty sequence. Its
60 synthetic inputs replace 60 real replays, while primary real exposure
remains fixed. This changes auxiliary content, not optimizer-update count.

Moving existing cargo reduced scheduled synthetic goods pixels from 400,549
to 173,773 and dark-goods pixels from 110,459 to 35,420. Full-schedule dark
goods pixels including primary real inputs are 1,223,810 versus 1,267,616
in the fresh control. Class-normalized loss coefficients remain fixed; pixel
counts do not establish gradient influence or a unique causal explanation.

Evaluation darkness is native display luminance below 50. Training-exposure
darkness uses BOX-resampled 640x360 RGB; target resizing is NEAREST. Small
connected-reference regions are diagnostics, not annotated instances or
distance estimates. Their full counts and recall/miss metrics are preserved.

Verification: all 72 native development predictions independently reproduce
per-frame and aggregate confusion counts. Checkpoint strict-reload provenance,
all input counts, source/configuration hashes and all 517 protected artifacts
pass. Both fresh arms use the corrected 32-training/12-development cohort.
Original targets, retained weights and earlier experiments are preserved.

Paired native-pixel transitions pooled across the three seeds show 635,021 fewer
false goods pixels but 81,465 additional missed goods pixels. About 79.6% of net
additional misses lie in connected reference-goods regions larger than 4,096
native pixels; these regions are not object instances. Largest mean net added
misses occur in 000282, 000753, 000376 and 000188. The remaining failure therefore
extends beyond tiny/distant goods. This diagnoses effects, not their unique cause.

Next decision: retain the baseline and close this placement trial as unsuccessful.
Before another generation revision, compare representative large/wrapped loads
and stored-goods groups with the existing synthetic cargo in a focused appearance
and context review. Derive any new scene parameters only from training sources.
Preserve the accepted batch and reviewed references; no further generation or
annotation changes are part of this completed comparison.

Fresh control scores differ from the historical saved control. A separate
read-only audit found identical training row order, native RGB/target hashes,
input counts, initialization and common recorded runtime settings. The cause
of checkpoint differences remains unresolved. Historical scores are context;
the new candidate is judged against its fresh matched control. This check
does not prove full-run determinism or isolate a numerical cause.

Runtime: NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5070 Laptop GPU, float32, 640x360 training,
native 1280x720 scoring, batch size 4, four CPU threads and cuDNN disabled.
CUDA launch blocking is enabled. Per-run training/verification time and peak
allocated CUDA memory are recorded in each seed profile; no throughput or
scalability claim is inferred from this small trial.

Limitations: one training and one repeatedly used development trajectory,
dependent video frames, three seeds, model-assisted reviewed references and
a sparse authored aisle with repeated assets/materials. These results do not
establish statistical significance, independent-recording generalization,
physical sensor fidelity or production suitability. Locked test untouched.
