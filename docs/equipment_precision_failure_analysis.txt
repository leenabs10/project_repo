Equipment precision failure analysis and revision plan
====================================================

Scope and conclusion
This audit inspects the saved synthetic-data comparison and training coverage.
It does not train, generate, change annotations or select a replacement model.
The candidate removes many background false goods while losing real goods.
The demonstrated generation gap is positive cargo placement/occupied-rack context,
not a measured shortage of total dark-goods pixel exposure. A unique causal
mechanism is not established by this observational analysis.

The completed experiment remains47.05% ->51.92% IoU,49.70% ->58.40% precision,
90.07% ->83.30% recall against its fresh matched control on frozen targets.
The complete utility rule fails. The retained reporting model and original
source-bound results are preserved. Its reported50.84/54.48/88.74 scores remain
bound to the original revised references, not a newly corrected target cohort.

Direct comparison with reviewed TRAINING sources
Training examples contain occupied racks, mixed apparent goods sizes and complex
stock/support contacts;000383 includes the blue stairs partly occluding cartons.
All four current synthetic positives contain one large near cargo group against
mostly empty shelving. The old right-bay tyre view with upright/crop interruption
has disappeared; both current tyre positives are clear, near-left silhouettes.
That historical contrast is confounded, not an isolated causal experiment.
Real training masks contain293 tiny (<=256pixels) and220 small (257..4096pixels)
connected regions, accounting for10.20%ofgoods pixels; synthetic masks have zero
tiny/small foreground regions and four large groups. Components are not objects.
Nearest target resizing removes67 entire tiny regions containing268 native goods
pixels (~0.0084%of training goods). This does not measure boundary/thin-structure
or RGB-detail loss inside surviving regions and cannot explain broad misses alone.

One justified revision, with preconditions
1. Adjudicate only the specific person conflict in000376. Save a new source-bound
   correction after review, preserving other valid regions and all prior versions.
   Recount unchanged retained/control/candidate and historical-equipment predictions
   on the same intended reference revision before setting the next benchmark.
   Changed targets are not a model gain. Recompute the reference-dependent numeric
   historical dark-miss ceiling on that revision before freezing it; preserve the
   acceptance formula rather than blindly applying the old18.54% number.
2. Change ONE generation factor: cargo placement/occupied rack/bay composition.
   Use verified training-source shelf contexts and apparent-size/occlusion bounds.
   Keep registered meshes, dimensions, UV/materials, camera/intrinsics, lighting,
   negative equipment, eight captures and paired empty/loaded semantics fixed.
   Include visible smaller shelf groups and partial rack occlusion alongside near
   cargo. This tests context coverage; it does not claim to solve wrapped-material
   appearance. Unsupported assets/materials remain blocked pending qualification.
3. Keep the existing model/initialization/optimizer/loss/threshold/checkpoint policy
   and sampling influence fixed. Preserve480 primary+60 auxiliary/120 updates and
   14 tyre+16 carton+30 empty visits, with the same ordered capture correspondence.
   New job IDs alter hash ordering, so explicit validated schedule support or an
   equivalent verified mechanism is required before execution. Measure/freeze actual
   per-capture and dark-pixel exposure. Do not simultaneously restore20 tyre visits.
4. Validate support/contact/collisions, visible size, metric depth, RGB/label/instance
   correspondence and independent occluder rays. Review exact revised appearance
   and labels, record new dataset/job acceptance and admit a separate snapshot.
5. Run ONE frozen three-seed comparison against a fresh matched real-only control
   on the same qualified cohort. Require the complete +3ppIoU/precision gain/recall
   nonregression/background-FP reduction/dark-preservation rule. Report every seed,
   frame gain/regression and both pixel-weighted/frame-weighted metrics.
6. If all gates pass, freeze candidate/protocol before separate-recording evaluation;
   keep the locked test reserved for final selection. If the revision fails, stop
   expansion and reconsider synthesis before generating more similar scenes.
