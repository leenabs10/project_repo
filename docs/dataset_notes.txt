Date: October 5, 2026

Source: https://github.com/Viky397/TorWICDataset
Dataset root: data/raw/torwic_slam
Actual extracted paths have two repeated directory levels. The manifest preserves
these paths; no raw files were renamed or moved.

Confirmed sequence splits
Train: Aisle_CCW_Run_1/Aisle_CCW_Run_1, June 15, 2022, 1,136 frames.
Validation: Aisle_CW_Run_1/Aisle_CW_Run_1, June 23, 2022, 1,037 frames.
Test: Aisle_CCW/Aisle_CCW, October 12, 2022, 915 frames.
Dates confirmed by the user. Split by whole trajectory; no neighboring-frame shuffle.

Ingestion contract
RGB: uint8, 1280x720, left and right cameras.
Depth: uint16 PNG, raw values multiplied by 0.001 for metres. Zero pixels are
masked; floating-point depth uses NaN at invalid pixels.
Semantics: grayscale integer class IDs 0-15. Do not normalize these like RGB
or treat the colored visualization as a class-index target. Trajectory masks
are model predictions, not manual ground truth.
LiDAR: binary PCD, little-endian float32 x/y/z, no intensity field in checked
files. Filter nonfinite and zero XYZ points before statistics or projection.
Pose: timestamp followed by xyz and quaternion xyzw, associated with os_sensor.
Frame times: relative sequence times; use their actual values, not assumed 10 Hz.
IMU: raw seven-column streams. The core loader does not interpolate IMU.

Checks completed

First/middle/last samples from all three sequences decoded successfully for

Calibration interpretation
Camera poses relative to os_sensor must be inverted for LiDAR-to-camera use:
p_camera = R.T @ (p_os - t).
Beginning/middle/end training frames support this direction for both cameras.
Inverse-transform median axial-depth residuals: about 0.019-0.042 metres across
the tested distorted/undistorted projection variants. Direct-transform residuals:
about 0.82-3.07 metres. These are overlapping-pixel diagnostics, not independent
calibration error measurements. Distortion improves median residuals in these
samples but has mixed effects on the fraction within 0.10 m; preserve the
distortion parameters and validate the image convention before production use.
Occlusion, range limits, reflective surfaces and moving objects affect residuals.

Data caveats
Aisle_CCW has 158 frame intervals over 0.15 seconds, maximum about 0.402 seconds.
One backward timestamp interval occurs in its left IMU stream and in both
Aisle_CCW_Run_1 IMU streams. Preserve raw data; any sorting belongs in a loader
with explicit ordering and logging. The first three Aisle_CCW frames and first
two Aisle_CW_Run_1 frames precede the corresponding IMU time spans.
Positive depth coverage and usable LiDAR counts vary greatly between frames.
Do not generalize the first checked sample into a dataset-wide degradation rule.
