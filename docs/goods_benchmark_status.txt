Failure analysis
The completed diagnostic audit measures lower dark-goods auxiliary exposure,
uneven scene sampling and missing all-background scene coverage. Native dark-goods
miss rate rises from 26.45% to 40.41%; the background-only frame's false-positive
rate improves from 25.28% to 22.28%. These are associations, not isolated causes.
The source-preserving donor-quota ablation is now complete and also fails the
utility rule. The retained model, references and accepted scenes are unchanged. See docs/goods_failure_analysis.txt.

scorable training pixels; two frames have no identified goods. Training IoU is
94.42% versus 50.84% on development for the retained model. This is consistent
with overfitting/coverage limitations, without proving a unique cause or label error.

Nine bounded RGB-gain variants from six source frames received explicit

Nine of twelve requested RGB cases are admitted; three RGB rejections remain

brightness variation, not new scene geometry or calibrated sensor simulation.

Admission and comparison capability
The scene admission CLI verifies the canonical dataset, independent geometry,
task/cohort version and explicit per-sample appearance/label decisions before writing
a separate eligible snapshot. It preserves the original quarantined export and
copies complete qualification evidence. Pending decisions, altered files, unknown
versions and conflicting real/synthetic splits are rejected. Known fixtures remain
separate from customer data.

The scene loader replaces RGB and target together using the rendered visible labels;
it does not reuse a real target for a generated scene. A 20% deterministic substitution
schedule preserves source-slot, presentation and optimizer-update budgets. Evaluation
remains on the fixed real development cohort. The three-seed comparison fixture passes
training, reload and matched-budget checks. Eight accepted samples have passed
admission and native paired-loader checks. The customer scene arm is complete.

Qualification and measured utility
All eight scenes have explicit source-bound appearance/label acceptance. The
original exports remain quarantined; a separate admitted snapshot is training-
eligible. The matched three-seed comparison is complete and the candidate is
rejected: mean IoU 48.63% -> 48.50%, precision 51.48% -> 54.22%, recall
89.76% -> 82.71%. Recall decreases on all three seeds. Frame-mean IoU falls
42.00% -> 38.69%; five frames improve, six regress and one is unchanged.
All 72 native development masks independently reproduce the saved scores.

Each hybrid seed uses 356 real and 94 scene presentations within the fixed
450-presentation/120-update budget. Goods occupy 12.24% of executed supervised
pixels versus 14.84% in the control. Reduced real exposure, target coverage and
visual-domain differences are measured confounds, not a proven causal explanation.
The retained model is unchanged. See docs/scene_benchmark_results.txt for paired
seed results, diagnostics and coverage requirements. No existing annotation round
needs repeating because of these results. Independent held-out evaluation,
simulated LiDAR and calibrated physical fidelity remain open.
