Warehouse goods benchmark status

Reference dataset
The qualified reference cohort contains 30 training and 12 development samples.
Native geometry, source checksums, categorical IDs and PNG/RLE round-trips pass.
All 128 frozen reference artifacts retain their hashes. Training targets are
unchanged. Empty pallets and handling equipment are background, identifiable loads
are goods and unresolved regions remain ignored. Model-assisted corrections retain
anchoring and coverage limitations; independent annotation accuracy is not established.

Retained model
Mean goods IoU: 50.84% | Precision: 54.48% | Recall: 88.74%.
These are means across seeds 17/29/43 on the current 12-frame development cohort.
The retained model is unchanged. Scores on older reference versions are not directly
comparable. Annotation changes require a demonstrated target error, not a lower score.
The retained model configuration is preserved as a historical training definition;
its original reference-packet binding remains frozen.

Completed RGB-condition experiment
Configuration                 | Goods IoU | Precision | Recall
------------------------------|-----------|-----------|-------
Matched final-epoch real-only  | 49.96%    | 53.99%    | 87.09%
Real plus RGB conditions      | 50.70%    | 55.06%    | 86.63%

Nine accepted native RGB variants originate from six training frames. Every seed
uses 450 presentations and 120 updates: 404 real inputs plus 46 condition inputs,
with original targets preserved. All three hybrid checkpoints reload strictly and
36 native development predictions independently reproduce saved confusion counts.
The candidate is rejected for recall regression and does not replace the retained
model. These historical fixed-final experiment results are separate from the training
CLI's default best-development checkpoint policy. They do not establish scene-data
utility, statistical significance or held-out generalization.

Published scene capability
A portable cuboid scene contract and isolated Blender Cycles worker produce paired
1280x720 RGB, clean axial depth, validity, semantic and instance outputs. Seven
related captures cover empty equipment, compact/wide/tall loads, equipment occlusion,
a lateral viewpoint and a distant target. Source-bound surface catalogs retain map
hashes, license/source information, metric scale and color-space conventions.

Independent checks cover 1,792 analytic rays with complete instance agreement and
per-view p95 depth error below 0.00005 m. Target visibility and equipment occlusion
are measured from native labels/depth. These captures share one authored warehouse;
procedural geometry and limited diversity remain appearance limitations. Histogram
agreement is a diagnostic rather than qualification evidence.

Verification and qualification
The isolated publication snapshot passes 201 tests and 80 subtests. Validated
interfaces include scene/job bindings, payload integrity, task semantics, support
constraints, target visibility and RGB-condition sampling with unchanged real targets.
BlenderProc remains unavailable pending its separate renderer dependencies. Simulated
LiDAR, calibrated sensor noise, physical dynamics and remote execution are unsupported.

Scene appearance qualification remains pending, with no scene-generated training or
final held-out evaluation completed. Scene-data admission and a matched scene utility
comparison remain open. The published pipeline keeps unqualified scene outputs
training-ineligible and preserves earlier reports, annotations and retained models.
