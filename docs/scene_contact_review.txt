Training-supported scene contact qualification
=============================================

Revision
Geometry inspection identified 11-15 mm carton-stack gaps caused by stacking
asset bounding boxes instead of irregular mesh surfaces. The separate revision
translates nine cartons vertically across four views, leaving 0.2 mm clearance
between adjacent carton surfaces. Tyre geometry, source meshes, UV materials,
equipment, cameras, lighting and class IDs are unchanged. All eight RGB, depth
and categorical-label sets were rendered together from the revised job.

Verification
The revised job reproduces exactly from its original source and contact algorithm.
All 2,048 independent geometry rays match the rendered instance IDs, including
1,280 rays targeting goods and equipment meshes. Maximum per-view p95 axial-depth
residual is below 0.00004 m. Visible semantic pixels agree with source roles:
cartons and tyres are goods; empty pallets and handling crates are background.
Lattice openings preserve whichever surface is visible behind the equipment.
The empty-equipment view contains shelf stock and is not an all-negative image.

Mean foreground coverage is 12.24%; training-derived low/high and median coverage
gates pass. The complete regression suite passes 233 tests and 112 subtests.
These checks establish correspondence and coverage, not appearance acceptance,
physical stability, sensor fidelity or downstream model improvement.

Reference integrity
The required frozen cohort was located and independently verified: all 128
artifacts match profile d4928f47f069b0ae29ce1c7dae9282d54e8bbb3a4b8944e291ce8422a4d39468.
The earlier mismatch referred to an older local pointer, not missing or modified
frozen references. No annotation version was substituted or overwritten.

Qualification status
All eight revised exports received explicit appearance and label acceptance and
passed source-bound admission and native paired-loader checks. Their separate
protocol was frozen before training; the original batch and protocol are intact.
The completed three-seed comparison fails its utility rule: IoU 49.66% -> 48.23%,
precision 53.02% -> 51.52%, recall 88.80% -> 88.55%. All 72 native development
predictions were independently recounted. Scene expansion is paused. The retained
model is unchanged; no promotion or held-out test evaluation has occurred.
Appearance/geometry qualification does not imply training benefit.

One authored aisle, repeated damage/textures, uniform lighting, unsupported
transparent wrapping and unverified load stability remain limitations. Vertical
contact alone does not establish a physically stable stack.
