What to paint, and how to review it

The examples use packet images and categorical masks to illustrate the annotation standard. Preserve source geometry, class definitions and genuine uncertainty.

Use these three classes

Colour and editor selectionCover these pixelsDo not do this
Goods (1)Visible identifiable loads, cartons, crates, wrapped stock and stored goods, including loads carried on robots.Do not select a whole rectangular area around an object. Fill its visible surfaces, following its outline.
Background (0)Robot bodies, people, lifts, shelf structure, support pallets, empty pallets/handling containers, barriers, floor, walls, ceiling and visible gaps.Do not call equipment goods because it is wooden or brown. Separate the load from its supporting equipment.
Ignore (255)Genuinely unclear identity, unresolvable blur or uncertain boundaries.Do not infer hidden contents or leave clear floor/clear goods unanswered merely to reach a coverage percentage.

Some wooden crates enclose identifiable cargo; empty handling crates/containers are background. Inspect their role in RGB. If it cannot be established, use Ignore. Colour in the photograph is never the class rule.

Review one image at a time

  1. Open RGB only. Identify the visible goods and supporting equipment before looking at the draft labels.
  2. Switch to RGB + all classes. Fill missed goods surfaces with Goods. Paint mistaken goods on equipment as Background.
  3. Use a larger brush inside a clear surface, then a small brush near its outline. Example starting radii: 20 native pixels inside a large object, 2 near an edge. Zoom to 200% for precision.
  4. Keep visible gaps and obstructions in their own class. Do not draw goods behind a person, rack or robot that blocks them.
  5. Inspect orange regions: resolve the clear ones; leave genuine uncertainty ignored. There is no target pink percentage. Coverage is not an accuracy score.
  6. Check Classes only for stray patches, then check RGB again. Scroll through the entire image. Use Undo stroke for mistakes.
  7. Only after your inspection, enter your reviewer name, tick the complete-image confirmation and select Save validated reviewed version. Check the success message. Export reviewed JSON is also available.

Saving validates dimensions, IDs, source bindings and format. It does not establish independent semantic accuracy or automatically admit training data.
